About this tool
AssessmentInfo

Why I made this.

My experiences with projects and clients have taught me to look beyond the brief. A project can be exciting and still be the wrong commitment if the budget, payment expectations or working relationship do not align.

I made this tool to give myself a clearer way to assess those things before committing my time. Can the client afford the work? Will they make time for it? Who makes the decisions? How do they communicate, handle disagreement and treat other people?

Those conversations matter as much as the deliverables. This rubric brings them into one place, so I can make a considered decision instead of relying only on enthusiasm, urgency or a good first impression.

What does it assess?

The assessment covers budget, payment reliability, availability, urgency, decision-making, the working relationship and respect. Its 22 questions focus on what a client says, agrees to and does. Each result highlights the strengths, concerns and unanswered questions to consider before moving forward.

How should I use the result?

Complete it after an initial conversation, using specific examples where you can. Choose “Not sure yet” when you need more information. A useful next step may be another conversation, a smaller scope, clearer payment terms or deciding the project is not a fit.

The score is a guide to a decision, not a prediction of how someone will behave. Serious concerns about payment, threats or disrespect can outweigh a strong total. The aim is to protect the time and attention needed to do good work, and to begin with expectations both sides understand.

Where are my assessments saved?

Saved assessments stay in the browser you use. They are not sent to a server or synced between devices. Save the result as a PDF or download the assessment HTML if you want to keep a separate copy.
